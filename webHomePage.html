Frequently asked question

Portfolio actually free, or is it just a trial?
+
You can build and publish a real portfolio website for free, with no time limit and no credit card required. When you want to add a custom domain or use our advanced business tools, you can easily upgrade to a paid plan.
Will my portfolio website look good on mobile devices?
+
Yes. Every MyPortfolio template is fully responsive and automatically adapts to all screen sizes, from desktop to tablet to mobile.
Am I locked into a single portfolio template once I start?
+
Yes. Every MyPortfolio template is fully responsive and automatically adapts to all screen sizes, from desktop to tablet to mobile.
Do I need coding or design skills?
+
No. MyPortfolio is a visual editor that runs in your browser. You build your site by moving things around and seeing changes in real time. No downloads, no code, no technical knowledge needed.
How does the 0% commission work for selling online?
+
MyPortfolio does not take any commission on your sales. You keep 100% of your revenue when selling products, digital files, bookings, or appointments through your portfolio.
What is the difference between the free and paid plans?
+
The free plan gives you a real portfolio website with 5 pages, 30 images, and gallery layouts. Paid plans add a custom domain, more storage, e-commerce, bookings, invoicing, custom email, and analytics. Start free and upgrade when you need more.
