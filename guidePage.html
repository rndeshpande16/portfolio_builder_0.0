How to Create Your Portfolio

01. Pick Your Elements:

Drag elements from the sidebar directly into the editor to start building your layout.

02. Personalize:

Click on any tab on subpage nav and a window will open to edit the contents of it

03. Style It:

Use the global styling tools to change colors, fonts. across your entire site.

04. Preview:

Check how your portfolio looks on mobile, tablet, and desktop views with one click.

05. Download:

Once you're happy, hit download the button!
